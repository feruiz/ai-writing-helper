Brand Voice Creator

Build your brand's voice the way you build a game character. Every choice rewrites the text on the right and becomes a rule in the guide.

Ready-made characters

Identity

The brand

What the sliders can't say. All optional and short: what you fill in goes near the top of the guide, and empty fields stay out.

Situations

The voice stays and the tone shifts with the situation. Switch off the ones the brand doesn't write for, or add its own, with the shifts it needs. They show up in the tabs, in the tags of the examples, in the test and in the guide.

Foundation

Who speaks, who it talks to and in what role. Like race and class: it shapes everything else.

Temperament

Each position becomes a concrete rule. The rule shows up under the slider.

Finish

Approved examples

Real texts the brand has approved. They become the guide's examples, which is what an AI imitates most. Tag each one with the situation it was written for.

Rejected texts

Texts you turned down, each with the reason. The guide shows the latest few under "Not like this" so the model avoids the pattern the reason names, not the subject. A text without a reason stays out of the guide.

        Build the voice in your AI chat

        Works in any chat: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or another. This site never talks to the AI and needs no key: you copy a request, have the conversation there, and bring the result back here.

        1. 1. Start the conversation

          Copy the request and paste it in a new conversation. The AI asks for real texts from the brand and a few questions, places the voice on the same ruler as the sliders, shows you the proposal, and ends with a block of settings.

          Talk in
          See the request

        2. 2. Bring the result back

          Paste the AI's reply with the block of settings, or the whole conversation: the site finds the latest block in it. The voice changes here, and you can undo it.

        3. 3. Keep going

          Changed the sliders or the fields here? Copy the voice as it is now and paste it in the same conversation, so the AI carries on from it. To hear the voice on one situation, use "Copy the request for your AI" under the text.
