Brand Voice Creator

Build your brand's voice the way you build a game character. Every choice rewrites the text on the right and becomes a rule in the guide.

Ready-made characters

Identity

The brand

What the sliders can't say. All optional and short: what you fill in goes near the top of the guide, and empty fields stay out.

Situations

The voice stays and the tone shifts with the situation. Switch off the ones the brand doesn't write for, or add its own, with the shifts it needs. They show up in the tabs, in the tags of the examples, in the test and in the guide.

Foundation

Who speaks, who it talks to and in what role. Like race and class: it shapes everything else.

Temperament

Each position becomes a concrete rule. The rule shows up under the slider.

Finish

Approved examples

Real texts the brand has approved. They become the guide's examples, which is what an AI imitates most. Tag each one with the situation it was written for.

Rejected texts

Texts you turned down, each with the reason. The guide shows the latest few under "Not like this" so the model avoids the pattern the reason names, not the subject. A text without a reason stays out of the guide.

With Claude (optional)

To write with AI and diagnose texts. Your key goes straight from your browser to Anthropic and never passes through any other server. Prefer a test key with a spending limit.

        Diagnose my texts

        Paste one to three real, approved texts from the brand, in English or Portuguese, ideally of different kinds (a post, an email, a reply). Claude places the sliders, and lists the habits it can point to in the texts (how they open and close, the words they reach for, what they never do), each with an excerpt as evidence; you tick the ones to add to the brand fields. Requires the API key.

        Test the guide

        Write one to three short requests for texts the brand would really need, about its own business and unlike the approved examples. Claude writes each one with the guide as its only instructions. A second call then reads the texts blind, without the guide or your settings, and places the sliders. The closer that reading lands to your settings, the better the guide carries the voice. Requires the API key.
